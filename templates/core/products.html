Common questions

Things clients ask before signing.

Which Odoo version should I pick? +

Odoo 17 (latest LTS) is the default. I also support v16 and v15, and run migrations from v12–v14. If you're starting fresh today, go with v17 Community or Enterprise depending on whether you need the studio/web/knowledge apps.

How long until we're live? +

Single-module rollout (POS, Accounting, Inventory) typically goes live in 2–4 weeks. Multi-module ERP with custom integrations lands in 6–10 weeks. You see working software on a staging server every week — no big-bang reveal at the end.

What does it cost? +

It depends on scope. A single POS rollout with payment integration lands around $3–6K. A full 4–6 module ERP with custom workflows lands $15–35K. Open the pricing calculator for a rough range, or send a brief for a fixed quote.

Do I need to sign an NDA first? +

Yes — I sign your mutual NDA before the first scoping call. Work happens on a dedicated staging server you provision, or behind your VPN. Role-based access, audit logs, encrypted-at-rest by default.

What happens after go-live? +

Every project includes 30 days of post-launch support — bug fixes, user questions, minor adjustments. After that, a monthly retainer or per-task engagement. You'll get a CI test suite, recorded walkthroughs, and full documentation handoff at the end of every project.
